# UI System

## Brand

AAZADI uses a geometric institutional monogram: a restrained `A` inside an obsidian square, with a peach baseline. The mark suggests constitutional structure, archive, and public record without relying on fists, microphones, or newspaper symbols.

## Color Tokens

- `obsidian`: permanence and archival seriousness.
- `maroon`: authority accent used for trust indicators, rules, and primary actions.
- `peach`: humane warmth against dark institutional surfaces.
- `paper`: warm off-white reading background.
- `cream`: document-like foreground and panel surfaces.
- `muted`: long-form support text.

## Typography

- Editorial serif stack for headlines and article body.
- Modern sans stack for interface labels, metadata, navigation, and forms.
- Headlines are bold and compact; body text uses generous line-height for mobile reading.

## Components

- `ArticleCard`: investigation previews with integrity indicators.
- `PublicPulse`: structured civic awareness layer for India, world, states, cities, and issue categories.
- `CivicMapPanel`: lightweight geographic civic awareness surface.
- `EvidenceLedger`: visible evidence records with uploader, type, timestamp, and verification state.
- `IntegrityPanel`: sticky article trust sidebar.
- `GovernanceCard`: public institutional decision records.
- `CorrectionCard`: public correction history.
- `JournalistCard`: reporter trust profile.
- `StructuredDiscussion`: correction suggestions, evidence challenges, contextual additions, and public discussion.

## Interaction Principles

- No infinite feeds.
- No popularity or virality mechanics.
- Pulse records are classified, curated, and finite.
- Internal surfaces use Newsroom, Civic Desk, Public Record System, Evidence Vault, and Governance Chamber language.
- Subtle hover states only.
- Cards are used for repeated records and tool panels, with radius capped at 8px.
- Mobile navigation remains lightweight and scrollable rather than JS-heavy.
